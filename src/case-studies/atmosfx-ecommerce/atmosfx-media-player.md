## Executive Summary
Redesigned the media player into a predictable, guided playlist experience that reduced confusion and support load while driving peak-season engagement.

## Challenge
High friction during playlist creation and playback made seasonal campaigns error‑prone.

## Solution
Guided Create→Preview→Playlists flow with device‑aware defaults.

## Results
Support volume dropped; seasonal conversion and satisfaction improved.

---

## Discovery
Reduce seasonal support costs; limited hardware resources.

## Research
Customer interviews + support ticket analysis to pinpoint failure patterns.

## Synthesis
Personas: ‘Holiday Host’, ‘DIY Decorator’. Journey map of setup day.

## Strategy
Reassure, preview before commit, graceful fallback.

## Information Architecture
Three primary modes: Library, Playlists, Now Playing.

## Interaction Design
Prototypes in Axure; progress indicators; empty‑state guidance.

## Visual Design
Tokenized capsule CTAs; accessible contrast; seasonal accents.

## Validation
Two moderated rounds; error‑recovery tasks; remote tests with real content.

## Implementation
Specs + tokens delivered; QA checklist for hardware quirks.

## Outcomes & Reflection
Next: smart recommendations from purchased packs.
