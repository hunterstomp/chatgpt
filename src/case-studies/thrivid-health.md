## Executive Summary
Flows for symptom logging, care plans, and clinician dashboards.

## Challenge
Fragmented data across visits.

## Solution
Unified timeline; structured inputs; shared plans.

## Results
Higher adherence and insight velocity.

---

## Discovery
Adherence; privacy/regulatory constraints.

## Research
Interviews; chart audits.

## Synthesis
Personas: patient, clinician, coordinator.

## Strategy
Minimize input; maximize signal.

## Information Architecture
Timeline + tasks + alerts.

## Interaction Design
Accessible forms; autosave.

## Visual Design
Soothing palette; clear affordances.

## Validation
Pilot; pre/post metrics.

## Implementation
FHIR‑friendly field mapping.

## Outcomes & Reflection
Integrations with wearables.
