## Executive Summary
Fewer steps + clarified pricing minimized choice paralysis.

## Challenge
Users stalled comparing plans, promos, devices.

## Solution
Opinionated defaults; comparison chips; transparent costs.

## Results
Better throughput and accessory attach.

---

## Discovery
Throughput; promo complexity constraint.

## Research
Friction logs + replays.

## Synthesis
Spec‑driven vs price‑driven deciders.

## Strategy
Guide to ‘good enough’; total cost early.

## Information Architecture
Device→plan→checkout linearized.

## Interaction Design
Chip comparison model.

## Visual Design
Capsule CTAs; accessible tables.

## Validation
Quant/qual post‑launch.

## Implementation
Reusable component cards.

## Outcomes & Reflection
Simplify trade‑in and fees.
