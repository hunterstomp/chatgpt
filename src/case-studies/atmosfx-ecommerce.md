## Executive Summary
Optimized browse→bundle→checkout path. Clarified pack compatibility and added contextual upsells.

## Challenge
Users abandoned carts when unsure about compatibility or what a pack included.

## Solution
Comparison tables, compatibility chips, contextual add‑ons tied to playlist intent.

## Results
Fewer pre‑purchase questions; higher AOV during peak months.

---

## Discovery
Increase AOV; legacy CMS constraints.

## Research
Support queries + on‑site search logs.

## Synthesis
‘Party Planner’ vs ‘Collector’ behaviors.

## Strategy
Bundle first; remove uncertainty with inline examples.

## Information Architecture
Consolidated categories; filters for projector/setup.

## Interaction Design
Bundle builder prototype; inline loops.

## Visual Design
Brand palette; capsule CTAs.

## Validation
Usability sessions on bundle comprehension.

## Implementation
Annotated table patterns for dev handoff.

## Outcomes & Reflection
Track playlist→pack attach rate.
