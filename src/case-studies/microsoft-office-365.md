## Executive Summary
Systemized flows and prototypes reduced first‑run confusion and surfaced value early.

## Challenge
New users didn’t know where to start; value felt abstract.

## Solution
Guided tours, cross‑app suggestions, and templates.

## Results
Improved activation and template usage KPIs.

---

## Discovery
Activation + template adoption; many teams involved.

## Research
Telemetry + interviews with new tenants.

## Synthesis
Jobs to be done mapped to entry points.

## Strategy
Value‑first paths; reduce blank‑state anxiety.

## Information Architecture
Unified hub; recent + recommended surfaces.

## Interaction Design
Cross‑app prototypes; keyboard/focus fidelity.

## Visual Design
MS brand; WCAG AA tokens.

## Validation
A/B hints vs tours.

## Implementation
Tokens + slots spec for teams.

## Outcomes & Reflection
Usage‑based personalization next.
