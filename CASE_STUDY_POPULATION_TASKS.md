## Image Processing Workflow

### For Each Case Study:
1. **Image Discovery**: Scan client folder for relevant images
2. **Computer Vision Analysis**: Use AI to categorize images by UX process step
3. **SEO-Friendly Naming**: Rename images with descriptive, accessible names
4. **WebP Conversion**: Convert all images to WebP format for optimization
5. **Thumbnail Generation**: Create optimized thumbnails for galleries
6. **Full-Size Optimization**: Optimize full-size images for lightbox/carousel
7. **Frontmatter Integration**: Update case study markdown with image arrays

### Image Categories (10-Step UX Process):
1. **Discovery & Research** (01-05)
2. **User Research & Personas** (06-10)
3. **Analysis & Synthesis** (11-15)
4. **Information Architecture** (16-20)
5. **Wireframing & Prototyping** (21-25)
6. **Visual Design & UI** (26-30)
7. **Testing & Validation** (31-35)
8. **Implementation & Handoff** (36-40)
9. **Results & Impact** (41-45)
10. **Process Documentation** (46-50)

## Content Guidelines

### Case Study Structure:
- **Objective**: Clear project goals and business context
- **My Role**: Specific responsibilities and contributions
- **Constraints**: Timeline, technical, and business limitations
- **Process**: 10-step UX process with images
- **Results**: Quantifiable outcomes and impact
- **Problem**: Original challenge and user needs
- **Role & Responsibilities**: Detailed breakdown by phase
- **Outcomes**: Key results and stakeholder feedback

### SEO Optimization:
- Descriptive image alt text
- Comprehensive captions
- Proper heading structure
- Meta descriptions
- Relevant tags and categories
