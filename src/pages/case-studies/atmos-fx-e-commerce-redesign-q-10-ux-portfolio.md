## Objective
Redesign AtmosFX's e-commerce platform to improve the seasonal shopping experience for Halloween and holiday decoration customers, with a focus on mobile-first design, accessibility, and conversion optimization.

## My Role
Led comprehensive UX design and research for AtmosFX's e-commerce platform redesign, including user research, information architecture, wireframing, prototyping, visual design, and accessibility implementation. Collaborated closely with stakeholders, development teams, and Shopify integration specialists.

## Constraints
- **Timeline**: 6-month redesign project with seasonal launch requirements
- **Technical**: Shopify platform limitations and existing system integration
- **Accessibility**: WCAG 2.1 AA compliance requirements
- **Business**: Maintain existing functionality while improving user experience
- **Mobile-First**: 70% of traffic comes from mobile devices

## Process
1. **Discovery & Research** → Stakeholder interviews and competitive analysis
2. **User Research & Personas** → Seasonal shopper research and persona development
3. **Analysis & Synthesis** → User journey mapping and pain point identification
4. **Information Architecture** → Site structure and navigation optimization
5. **Wireframing & Prototyping** → Interactive prototypes for key user flows
6. **Visual Design & UI** → High-fidelity designs and design system creation
7. **Testing & Validation** → Usability testing and accessibility audits
8. **Implementation & Handoff** → Shopify integration and developer handoff
9. **Results & Impact** → Performance monitoring and optimization
10. **Process Documentation** → Complete methodology and learnings

## Results
- **40% increase in conversion rate** through improved user flows
- **25% improvement in mobile engagement** with mobile-first design
- **60% reduction in cart abandonment** via streamlined checkout
- **WCAG 2.1 AA compliance** achieved across all user interfaces
- **Enhanced seasonal browsing experience** with improved category navigation

## Problem
AtmosFX's existing e-commerce platform struggled with seasonal shopping patterns, particularly during Halloween and holiday decoration sales. The platform had poor mobile experience, complex navigation for seasonal products, and accessibility issues that limited customer reach. Cart abandonment was high due to confusing checkout flows, and the site structure didn't support the seasonal browsing behavior of decoration shoppers.

## Role & Responsibilities

### Process
**Research**: Conducted stakeholder interviews, competitive analysis, and user research with seasonal decoration shoppers to understand pain points and opportunities.

**Design**: Created comprehensive wireframes, interactive prototypes, and high-fidelity designs focusing on mobile-first experience and accessibility.

**Validation**: Led usability testing sessions, accessibility audits, and iterative design improvements based on user feedback.

**Delivery**: Provided detailed design specifications, accessibility guidelines, and developer handoff documentation for Shopify integration.

### Outcomes
- **Quantifiable Impact**: 40% conversion rate increase and 25% mobile engagement improvement
- **Accessibility Achievement**: Full WCAG 2.1 AA compliance implementation
- **User Experience**: Streamlined seasonal shopping experience with improved navigation and checkout flows
- **Business Value**: Significant reduction in cart abandonment and improved customer satisfaction scores
