## Executive Summary
Plan picker clarifying daily vs monthly costs, coverage, and activation timing.

## Challenge
Users feared bill shock; unclear eligibility across lines.

## Solution
Line toggles, real‑time cost preview, coverage hints.

## Results
Higher opt‑in; fewer post‑trip support calls.

---

## Discovery
Increase roaming attach; compliance constraints.

## Research
Call drivers; competitors’ day‑pass patterns.

## Synthesis
Personas: Business Traveler, Family Trip Planner.

## Strategy
Expose total trip cost early; default safe.

## Information Architecture
Wizard flow + summary sheet; QR activation handoff.

## Interaction Design
Edge cases for multi‑line accounts.

## Visual Design
Contrast‑safe cards; capsule CTAs.

## Validation
Remote unmoderated comprehension tests.

## Implementation
APIs for eligibility checks.

## Outcomes & Reflection
Post‑trip confirmation survey.
