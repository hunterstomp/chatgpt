## Objective

Design an intuitive user experience for AT&T's international roaming services, making it easier for customers to understand and select appropriate roaming plans for their international travel needs.

## Challenge

International roaming is a complex service with many variables including different countries, data plans, and usage patterns. Users often struggle to understand their options and make informed decisions.

## Solution

Created a streamlined interface that guides users through the roaming plan selection process with clear visual hierarchy and simplified decision-making flows.

## Process

- User research to understand pain points
- Wireframing and prototyping
- User testing and iteration
- Final design implementation

## Results

The redesigned interface significantly improved user satisfaction and reduced support calls related to international roaming questions.
