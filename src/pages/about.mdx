I am Quentin Little, a Senior User Experience Designer with over two decades of professional experience in crafting intuitive, impactful digital products. My journey through the entire digital revolution, from tinkering with 386 processors to navigating today's AI landscape, has given me a unique, hands-on perspective on the intricate dance between humans and machines. This extensive background, coupled with a natural inclination for systematic analysis and meticulous attention to detail, fuels my passion for designing future-forward experiences.  
**About my career**My career has seen me lead UX initiatives across enterprise, mid-to-small businesses, and innovative startups, with transformative stints at Microsoft (including a company-wide Gold Star Award for executive vision work) and T-Mobile. I thrive in dynamic environments, adeptly bridging technical capabilities with human-centered design principles across diverse platforms including web, mobile, and television. I am particularly passionate about securing a lucrative UX design job in progressing tech, especially where UX can directly help with AI and UX integration, or in future-forward areas like AR/VR/MR/XR.

**What set****s me apart**

What sets me apart is my unwavering dedication to creating authentic, data-driven experiences. I believe in building fully interactive prototypes fed by real data streams, moving beyond static mockups to uncover genuine user insights and edge cases. This methodical approach ensures that designs are not only user-centered but also robust and reflective of real-world usage. For instance, at Disney, I prototyped a dashboard that ingested complex datasets, and at T-Mobile and AtmosFX, I developed internal content management systems that incorporated actual libraries and catalogs of data, allowing stakeholders to visualize and test the final product in real-world contexts. My work has consistently delivered measurable impact, such as achieving a 30% increase in e-commerce conversion rates for AtmosFX and improving task completion rates by 35% on Microsoft's Unified Action Tracker platform.

**Sabbatical**During a recent sabbatical, I immersed myself in AI to wrangle solutions built for UX and Machine Learning technologies, building prototypes and developing scripts to automate foundational tasks. This hands-on experience, combined with my proven ability to pioneer integrations with emerging technologies like AR, VR, ML, chatbots, and digital voice assistants at T-Mobile, positions me at the forefront of the evolving UX landscape. I am eager to apply this expertise to new opportunities in Bellevue, Seattle, WA, and surrounding areas, where I can contribute to the next wave of digital transformation.  
  
With all the tools I've learned I can bring a UX powerhouse of swift user-validated design and A.I automation to the challenges your company is facing.

**Other Hobbies**Beyond the pixels and code, I bring a unique creative perspective shaped by my hobbies as a circuit bender (building theremins and modifying MIDI synthesizers), and my appreciation for visual storytelling (influenced by directors like Michel Gondry and Spike Jonze) and data visualization (drawing inspiration from Edward Tufte, Iove all of his books and insights). I am a firm believer in continuous learning and knowledge sharing, striving to be an effective mentor and collaborator through a practical, measured approach to problem-solving. And yes, when I'm not pushing pixels or coding, you might find me paragliding over Seattle or performing Britney Spears' "Toxic" at karaoke or Life on Mars by David Bowie (with surprising accuracy, I might add!).
