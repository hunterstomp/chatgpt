## Executive Summary
Reusable layouts + charts kept focus on outcomes and accountability.

## Challenge
Dense data + diverse programs overwhelmed readers.

## Solution
Sectioned narratives; consistent charts; accessible color set.

## Results
Higher completion; better donor comprehension.

---

## Discovery
Comprehension; varied data quality.

## Research
Workshops; past report analytics.

## Synthesis
Skimmer vs deep‑reader modes.

## Strategy
Lead with outcomes; let details cascade.

## Information Architecture
Intro → Programs → Results → How funds worked.

## Interaction Design
Sticky TOC; footnote/figure links.

## Visual Design
WCAG‑AA charts; print styles.

## Validation
Editorial + screen‑reader reviews.

## Implementation
Data→chart pipeline guidance.

## Outcomes & Reflection
Open data annex next.
