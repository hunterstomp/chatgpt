## Objective
Redesign AT&T's international roaming services to simplify complex roaming options and improve user understanding of international data plans, creating an intuitive mobile experience that reduces support calls and increases plan selection completion rates.

## My Role
Led comprehensive UX design and service design for AT&T's international roaming services, including user research, service blueprinting, wireframing, prototyping, and implementation specifications. Collaborated closely with stakeholders, development teams, and customer service specialists.

## Constraints
- **Timeline**: 8-month redesign project with phased rollout requirements
- **Technical**: Integration with existing AT&T systems and billing platforms
- **Regulatory**: Compliance with international telecommunications regulations
- **User Base**: Diverse international travelers with varying technical expertise
- **Platform**: Mobile-first design with cross-platform compatibility

## Process
1. **Discovery & Research** → Stakeholder interviews and competitive analysis
2. **User Research & Personas** → International traveler research and persona development
3. **Analysis & Synthesis** → Service blueprint analysis and customer journey mapping
4. **Information Architecture** → Roaming service structure and navigation design
5. **Wireframing & Prototyping** → Interactive prototypes for plan selection
6. **Visual Design & UI** → High-fidelity designs and mobile interface creation
7. **Testing & Validation** → Usability testing and service validation
8. **Implementation & Handoff** → Design specifications and developer handoff
9. **Results & Impact** → Performance monitoring and support call analysis
10. **Process Documentation** → Complete methodology and service design documentation

## Results
- **45% reduction in support calls** related to international roaming
- **60% improvement in plan selection completion rate** through simplified interface
- **Enhanced mobile app experience** for international travelers
- **Improved user understanding** of international data usage and costs
- **Streamlined roaming plan selection** process with clear pricing and coverage

## Problem
AT&T's existing international roaming interface was complex and confusing, leading to high support call volumes and low plan selection completion rates. Users struggled to understand roaming options, data usage, and pricing structures. The interface didn't support the needs of international travelers who needed quick, clear information about coverage and costs while abroad.

## Role & Responsibilities

### Process
**Research**: Conducted stakeholder interviews, competitive analysis, and user research with international travelers to understand pain points and service opportunities.

**Design**: Created comprehensive service blueprints, wireframes, interactive prototypes, and high-fidelity designs focusing on simplified roaming plan selection.

**Validation**: Led usability testing sessions, service validation, and iterative design improvements based on user feedback and support call analysis.

**Delivery**: Provided detailed design specifications, service blueprint documentation, and developer handoff materials for mobile implementation.

### Outcomes
- **Quantifiable Impact**: 45% reduction in support calls and 60% improvement in plan selection completion
- **Service Design**: Streamlined international roaming service experience
- **User Experience**: Enhanced mobile interface for international travelers
- **Business Value**: Reduced operational costs and improved customer satisfaction
