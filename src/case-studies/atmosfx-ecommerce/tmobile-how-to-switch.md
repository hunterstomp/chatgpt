## Executive Summary
Progressive disclosure + status reduced fear around number porting.

## Challenge
Users feared downtime or losing their number.

## Solution
Progress tracker; contingency paths; tuned copy.

## Results
Faster completion; fewer abandonments.

---

## Discovery
Higher conversion; legacy porting APIs.

## Research
Interviews + support logs; benchmark competitors.

## Synthesis
Personas: Tech‑Anxious, Deal‑Hunter.

## Strategy
Reassure at every step; show status not jargon.

## Information Architecture
3‑step funnel with resume‑later.

## Interaction Design
Click‑through prototype to validate timing.

## Visual Design
Brand palette; capsule CTAs; strong contrast.

## Validation
10‑user moderated test; copy iteration.

## Implementation
Alt flows for failures; handoff notes.

## Outcomes & Reflection
Post‑port welcome checklist.
