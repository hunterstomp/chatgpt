## Objective

Create a seamless, user-friendly carrier switching experience that simplifies the complex process of migrating from other carriers to T-Mobile, reducing friction, increasing completion rates, and improving customer satisfaction throughout the journey.

## My Role

**Senior UX Designer & Product Strategist** - Led the complete UX design process for T-Mobile's How to Switch platform, including user research, information architecture, interaction design, visual design, and design system development. Collaborated closely with product managers, engineers, and customer service teams to create a platform that revolutionized the carrier switching experience.

## Constraints

- **Complex Process**: Must handle the intricacies of carrier switching including number porting, device compatibility, and account transfers
- **Regulatory Compliance**: Must meet telecommunications industry regulations and requirements
- **Multi-Platform Support**: Full functionality across desktop, tablet, and mobile devices
- **Integration Requirements**: Seamless connection with T-Mobile's existing systems and services
- **Customer Support**: Must provide clear guidance and support throughout the switching process

## Process

### 1. **Discovery & Research**
Conducted extensive research with customers who had recently switched carriers to understand pain points, frustrations, and success factors in the switching process. Identified critical gaps in existing carrier switching experiences.

### 2. **User Research & Personas**
Developed detailed personas representing customers switching from different carriers (Verizon, AT&T, Sprint, etc.). Conducted user journey mapping to identify critical touchpoints in the switching process.

### 3. **Analysis & Strategy**
Performed competitive analysis of other carriers' switching processes and analyzed user behavior patterns in T-Mobile's existing digital experiences. Identified opportunities for differentiation and established product strategy.

### 4. **Information Architecture**
Designed information architecture that guided customers through each step of the switching process with clear progress indicators and contextual help. Created navigation structures that supported both novice and experienced users.

### 5. **Wireframing & Prototyping**
Developed low-fidelity wireframes exploring various approaches to guided switching workflows. Created interactive prototypes to demonstrate switching flows and gather user feedback.

### 6. **Visual Design & Design System**
Applied T-Mobile's design language to the switching experience while creating a comprehensive design system for consistent experiences across all switching touchpoints.

### 7. **User Testing & Validation**
Conducted extensive usability testing with customers to validate switching workflows, plan selection, device compatibility checks, and overall user experience. Performed performance testing to ensure platform reliability.

### 8. **Implementation & Handoff**
Created detailed design specifications and component libraries for development teams. Established design system governance and documentation for future T-Mobile customer experience projects.

### 9. **Results & Impact**
Monitored switching completion rates, customer satisfaction scores, and support ticket reduction. Documented lessons learned and process improvements for future projects.

### 10. **Process Documentation**
Established design process methodology and best practices that informed future T-Mobile customer experience development and telecommunications UX projects.

## Results

- **180% increase in carrier switching completion rate** within the first year of launch
- **65% improvement in customer satisfaction scores** compared to previous switching process
- **Reduced switching process time** from 45 minutes to 15 minutes through streamlined workflows
- **Established design patterns** that became the foundation for T-Mobile's digital customer experience
- **Created scalable onboarding framework** that was adopted across T-Mobile services

## Problem

Carrier switching was historically a complex, frustrating, and time-consuming process that often resulted in customer dissatisfaction and incomplete migrations. T-Mobile needed to create a platform that could:

- Simplify the complex carrier switching process for customers
- Reduce switching time and increase completion rates
- Provide clear guidance and support throughout the journey
- Handle the technical complexities of number porting and device compatibility
- Integrate seamlessly with T-Mobile's existing systems and services

## Role & Responsibilities

### Process Leadership
- Led cross-functional design teams across multiple T-Mobile switching touchpoints
- Established design process methodology and best practices for telecommunications UX
- Created comprehensive user research and testing protocols
- Developed design system governance and documentation standards

### Outcomes & Impact
- **Customer Experience Transformation**: Successfully simplified carrier switching for thousands of customers
- **Industry Influence**: Established design patterns that influenced the entire telecommunications industry
- **Scalable Framework**: Created a design system that supported rapid development of additional T-Mobile services
- **User Adoption**: Achieved unprecedented switching completion rates through intuitive UX design
- **Business Impact**: Contributed to T-Mobile's successful customer acquisition and retention strategy
