## Objective
Create an engaging, interactive annual letter experience that turns complex data into clear, visual stories for a wide audience.

## My Role
Led UX for interactive storytelling: structure, flows, wireframes, high‑fidelity comps, and motion prototypes. Partnered with engineers on chart behaviors and performance.

## Constraints
Tight timeline, mixed data sources, and large accessibility surface (keyboard + screen reader friendly charts).

## Process
1. **Content modeling** → identified narrative beats and KPIs to highlight.
2. **Wireframes & flows** → defined scannable sections with progressive disclosure.
3. **Visualization patterns** → reusable chart templates and annotation styles.
4. **Proto & validation** → motion prototypes for transitions; quick hallway tests.
5. **Handoff** → design tokens + fig/spec patterns for dev parity.

## Results
- Stakeholders could publish data stories faster with consistent design patterns.
- Higher interaction with key modules (time‑on‑section ↑, drop‑offs ↓).
- Foundation gained a repeatable approach for future letters.

## Project Overview

This comprehensive UX design project for the Bill & Melinda Gates Foundation focused on creating compelling visual narratives that effectively communicate complex philanthropic initiatives to diverse audiences.

## Design Process

Our approach centered on understanding the Foundation's unique communication challenges and developing solutions that balance clarity with emotional impact. The design process included:

- **User Research**: Understanding diverse stakeholder needs and communication preferences
- **Information Architecture**: Organizing complex content into digestible, engaging narratives
- **Visual Design**: Creating compelling visual hierarchies that guide users through content
- **Prototyping & Testing**: Iterative refinement based on stakeholder feedback

## Key Deliverables

The project produced 17 comprehensive design deliverables, including:

- Annual letter designs that communicate complex initiatives clearly
- Portfolio showcases that highlight impact and outcomes
- Interactive elements that enhance user engagement
- Design systems that ensure consistency across all touchpoints

## Impact & Results

The redesigned materials achieved significant improvements in stakeholder communication and user engagement, demonstrating the power of thoughtful UX design in the non-profit sector.

## Key Takeaways

- **User-centered design approach** ensures content resonates with target audiences
- **Iterative prototyping and testing** leads to more effective final designs
- **Stakeholder collaboration** is essential for complex organizational projects
- **Measurable outcomes and results** demonstrate the value of UX investment
